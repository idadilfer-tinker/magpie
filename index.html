Why early adopters, and why not focus groups.

Forty years of innovation research points the same way: choose the right few people, talk to them one at a time, and stay close.

People don't always say what they mean.

Like an AI tool, people can give a confident answer that isn't quite true. They guess at what they'd do, tell you what you want to hear, or ask for a solution when what they have is a need.

Early adopters are different. They're the enthusiasts and tinkerers, often people who think differently, who live with the problem and can talk about it in depth. Their insight is gold, but it doesn't always arrive as a neat sentence.

That's why Magpie pairs the method with a trained AI agent that stays with them.

  • PROFILESBuilds a picture of each early adopter: what they need, and how they tend to express it.
  • STAYS ONStays with them for as long as your product and team need it, checking in at every iteration instead of surveying once.
  • DIGS DEEPERAsks the next question, past “faster horses” to the need underneath. Always disclosed as AI.
